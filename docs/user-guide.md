## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Navigate to the portal URL (e.g., `https://portal.ubi-platform.com`)
2. Click **"Create account"** on the login page
3. Fill in your details:
   - **Username** — 3–50 characters, letters, numbers, and underscores only
   - **Email address** — a valid email you have access to
   - **Password** — at least 12 characters, must include uppercase, lowercase, a number, and a special character (e.g., `@`, `!`, `#`)
4. Click **"Create Account"**
5. You'll be automatically logged in and redirected to your dashboard

> **Password example:** `MyPlatform@2026` ✅

### Logging In

1. Enter your username and password
2. Click **"Sign in"**
3. You'll be taken to your **Dashboard Overview**

Sessions last **24 hours**. You'll be prompted to sign in again after that.

### Trying the Demo

Not ready to create an account? Use the demo mode to explore the platform:

1. On the login page, click **"Try Demo (no account needed)"**
2. You'll be logged in as a demo user with sample data
3. Every screen shows a **Demo** badge so you always know you're in demo mode
4. All actions (claim UBI, apply for tasks, execute agents) are simulated — no real transactions occur

The **sidebar navigation** (left) gives you access to all sections. On mobile, tap the hamburger menu (☰) to open it.

## UBI Claims

**Location:** Dashboard → UBI Claims

Universal Basic Income is allocated periodically to active platform members.

### Checking Your Balance

Your current UBI balance is shown prominently at the top of the screen.

### Claiming Your UBI

1. Click the green **"Claim UBI"** button
2. If UBI is available, it will be added to your balance immediately
3. A success notification confirms the amount received

> **Note:** UBI becomes claimable on a schedule set by platform administrators. If you see "No UBI available to claim," check back later.

### Distribution History

Below the claim button, a table shows your complete UBI claim history:
- Date and time of each claim
- Amount received per claim
- Current status (confirmed / pending)

### Platform Statistics

The bottom of the page shows platform-wide stats:
- Total active users
- Total UBI distributed to date
- Total claims processed

## Task Marketplace

**Location:** Dashboard → Tasks

Earn UBI tokens by completing tasks. Tasks range from simple translations to complex research.

### Browsing Tasks

Use the **filter bar** at the top to narrow down tasks:

| Filter | Options |
|--------|---------|
| **Status** | Open, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled |
| **Category** | Translation, Research, Transcription, Data Entry, etc. |
| **Difficulty** | Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert |
| **Search** | Type keywords to search task titles and descriptions |

### Task Cards

Each task card shows:
- **Title and description**
- **Difficulty badge** (color-coded: green=easy → red=expert)
- **Reward amount** in UBI tokens
- **Slots available** (current participants / max participants)
- **Deadline** (if applicable)
- **Proof requirements** — what you need to submit

### Applying for a Task

1. Find a task that interests you
2. Review the proof requirements carefully
3. Click **"Apply"** on the task card
4. You'll receive a confirmation and the task moves to "In Progress"

> You can work on multiple tasks simultaneously.

### Submitting Your Work

1. Complete the task according to the proof requirements
2. Click **"Submit"** on the task card
3. Enter your proof (URL, text, or file reference as specified)
4. Click **"Submit Proof"**
5. Your submission is marked **"Pending Verification"**

### Verification and Rewards

A moderator will review your submission. Once approved:
- Your reward is automatically added to your Rewards balance
- The task shows as **"Completed"** in your history
- You receive a notification (if enabled)

If your submission is rejected, you may be able to resubmit after addressing the feedback.

## Treasury Management

**Location:** Dashboard → Treasury

Your treasury is like a savings account for your UBI tokens that earns yield.

### Checking Your Balance

Your current treasury balance is shown at the top of the screen along with your **current APY** (Annual Percentage Yield) and **estimated annual earnings**.

### Depositing Tokens

1. Click **"Deposit"**
2. Enter the amount to deposit (in UBI)
3. Review the transaction details
4. Click **"Confirm Deposit"**

Your tokens are immediately added to your treasury balance and begin earning yield.

### Withdrawing Tokens

1. Click **"Withdraw"**
2. Enter the amount to withdraw
3. Click **"Confirm Withdrawal"**

> You can only withdraw up to your available balance. Partial withdrawals are allowed.

### Yield Strategies

The **Yield Strategies** panel shows the active investment strategies for the treasury pool:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Strategy Name** | Name of the yield strategy |
| **Protocol** | Underlying protocol or mechanism |
| **APY** | Estimated annual percentage yield |
| **Risk Level** | Low / Medium / High |
| **Allocation** | % of pool allocated to this strategy |

Your yield is automatically compounded into your balance.

### Transaction History

A full paginated history of all deposits, withdrawals, and yield credits is shown at the bottom of the page.

## Rewards & Earnings

**Location:** Dashboard → Rewards

A unified view of all earnings across the platform.

### Reward Balance

Your total reward balance (accumulated but not yet spent or withdrawn) is shown at the top.

### Earnings Chart

An interactive bar chart shows your monthly earnings broken down by reward type:
- Task completions
- UBI claims

|--------|-------------|
| **Date** | When the reward was processed |

| **Status** | Confirmed / Pending |

## AI Agent Marketplace

**Location:** Dashboard → Agents

Discover and use AI agents that automate tasks and provide specialized capabilities.

### Browsing Agents

The agent grid displays all available agents. Each card shows:
- **Agent name and description**
- **Capability type** (text processing, code review, data analysis, etc.)
- **Pricing** (free or cost per call in UBI)
- **Success rate** and **total executions**

Use the **search bar** to find agents by name or description.

### Executing an Agent

1. Find an agent that meets your needs
2. Review the pricing (check "free" or the per-call cost)
3. Click **"Execute"**
4. A dialog appears — enter your input in the text field
5. Click **"Run Agent"**
6. Wait for the result (usually a few seconds)
7. The output appears in the dialog

> Agent execution costs (if any) are deducted from your Rewards balance.

### Recent Executions

Your recent executions are tracked in the **"Recent Executions"** table:
- Agent name
- Input provided
- Duration (milliseconds)
- Cost (UBI tokens)
- Timestamp

## Troubleshooting

### "Invalid credentials" on login
- Check your username and password (passwords are case-sensitive)
- Ensure Caps Lock is not on
- If you've forgotten your password, contact your platform administrator

### UBI balance shows 0
- Ensure you've claimed at least once using the **"Claim UBI"** button
- Check that your account is active (contact admin if unsure)

### "No UBI available to claim"
- UBI is distributed on a schedule; check back at the next distribution period
- Your account must be in "active" status

### Task application fails
- The task may already be full (max participants reached)
- You may already be assigned to this task

### Treasury withdrawal fails with "Insufficient balance"
- Ensure the withdrawal amount doesn't exceed your treasury balance
- Note that UBI rewards balance and treasury balance are separate

### Page shows loading spinner indefinitely
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page (F5 or Ctrl+R)
- If the backend API is down, try clicking "Try Demo" to use the platform in demo mode

### Agent execution returns an error
- The agent endpoint may be temporarily unavailable
- Try again in a few moments
- If the problem persists, the agent may have been deactivated by an administrator
